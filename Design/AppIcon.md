# Rewind app icon

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, then exported as an opaque 1024 × 1024 RGB PNG for Xcode’s AppIcon asset catalog. The app icon is original generated artwork, separate from the attributed in-app 3D model.

Saved asset: `Rewind/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png`

## Final generation prompt

Create one polished native iOS app icon artwork for Rewind, a minimal tactile cassette collection. Square 1024x1024 opaque full-bleed warm paper background #F3F0E9, no rounded outer corners because iOS applies its own mask. A single beautiful retro cassette centered, viewed nearly front on with a slight 3D perspective and gentle lean, occupies 76 percent of icon width. Cassette is warm charcoal and translucent amber plastic, a bold burnt orange #F36B3F and cream label, two clear ivory reel hubs with dark centers. Simplify into a highly legible premium physical object, softly modeled edges, subtle surface texture, very restrained soft shadow underneath. Editorial industrial design, nostalgic but fresh, consistent warm paper aesthetic. No words, letters, numbers, logos, play-button overlays, interface elements, border, mockup, device, or extra objects. Strong silhouette, two hubs unmistakably visible, harmonious generous padding, icon should remain readable at 60 pixels.
