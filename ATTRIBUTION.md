The original USDZ is preserved unchanged. Original.png is a lossless PNG conversion of the embedded JPEG. SuperTape.png and Vintage.png are adapted color versions of that atlas, made for Rewind. AnimatedCassette.usdz is a derivative: the original mesh is clipped into a fixed shell and two pivoted inner reel sections. Cut vertices interpolate the original UVs; source artwork, dimensions and transform hierarchy are retained. The source USDZ remains unchanged. Attribution and licence links are available in the app’s info sheet. No endorsement by the original creator is implied.

## Interaction sound

TapeClick.wav was procedurally synthesized for Rewind using Tools/make_click.py. No third-party audio samples or recordings were used.

## Side B adaptations

OriginalSideB.png, SuperTapeSideB.png and VintageSideB.png adapt the corresponding source-derived atlases with a B mark and original handwritten-style notes. The derivative USDZ also separates back-facing planar surfaces and reflects their horizontal UVs for readable back-side lettering. These changes retain the original model attribution and CC BY 4.0 reference above.
